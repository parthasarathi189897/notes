# Sparky Support for Homepage

> [!abstract] Summary
> Integrate Sparky AI support into the Sam's Club Homepage to enable conversational commerce, personalized assistance, and seamless member engagement from the primary landing surface. Members will be able to interact with Sparky for product discovery, membership questions, and personalized recommendations directly from the homepage.
