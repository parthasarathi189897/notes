> [!abstract] Summary
> Enable WCP (Walmart Commerce Platform) powered same-day delivery for Sam's Club members in the San Francisco market. High-density urban area with strong demand but limited same-day delivery coverage. $17M+ annualized GMV aligned with broader WCP slot selection initiative. Q2 target.
