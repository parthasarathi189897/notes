# Shoppable List

> [!abstract] Summary
> Enable a Shoppable List experience on Content Pages that allows Sam's Club members to view recipes and purchase ingredients in a single action. Drives cross-category discovery and incremental GMV ($10M target, $50M annualized at scale). Addresses gap vs. leading grocers who offer curated catering bundles simplifying meal planning.

## ⚠️ Risks & Blockers

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|:-----|:-------|:-----------|
| Content curation at scale (recipes, ingredient mapping) | Medium | Start with top 20 recipes; leverage existing recipe content partnerships |
| Ingredient-to-SKU mapping accuracy | High | Use catalog search API with fuzzy matching; manual curation for launch set |
| Below-the-line prioritization risk | Medium | Build lightweight POC to validate member engagement before full investment |
