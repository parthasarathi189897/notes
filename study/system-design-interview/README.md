## 🔁 Weekly rhythm

```
MON-FRI (commute/walk)  — Watch ByteByteGo video for this chapter (15-20 min, passive)
SATURDAY (after AI study) — 30 min: read chapter section OR draw diagram from memory
SUNDAY                    — Mark tracker (1 min)
```

**Rule:** Watch the video BEFORE reading the chapter. Animation gives you a mental scaffold; the book fills in depth.
